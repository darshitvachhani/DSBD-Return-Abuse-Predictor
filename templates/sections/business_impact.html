13. Business Impact, Financial ROI & Strategic Policy Playbook Executive Strategy
Checkpoint Mechanism — From Risk Score to Economic Action
Core Recommendation Architecture

The ML model should NOT independently decide whether a customer is an abuser. Instead, every return passes through a structured sequence of economic, behavioural, ML, and operational checkpoints designed to maximize net business value.

01 · Economic Viability
Is the return worth physically processing?
  • Logistics cost ≈ £13 (£7 freight + £6 handling).
  • If value ≤ £15 AND not a chronic abuser, avoid return burn.
Action: Keep Item / Store Credit
02 · LTV & Trust Shield
Could unnecessary friction cost more than fraud?
  • Top 20% Net LTV + Return frequency below 80th percentile.
  • History: tenure, valid returns, disputes.
Action: Protect VIP from Hard Friction
03 · ML Risk Score
How likely is this return to be abusive?
  • Champion: Bagging Ensemble (60 trees).
  • TF-IDF text + behavioral features. Output: P(Abuse|X).
Note: Model recall = 50.47% ≠ intercept rate (68%).
04 · Dynamic Economic Gate
What intervention level is justified?
GREEN (P < 0.35) Zero/Low friction, instant refund.
AMBER (0.35-0.65) Photo verification, £3.99 fee (waived on store credit).
RED (P ≥ 0.65) Refund hold & inspection. (High-risk return, not permanent label).
05 · Warehouse Verification
How much physical inspection is needed?
  • Green: Automated barcode restock.
  • Amber: Standard tag & condition QC.
  • Red: Forensic check (UV light, weight, 360° seals).
CONFIRMED OUTCOME → MODEL LEARNING
Economic Decision Logic & Threshold Optimization

Expected Fraud Loss = P(Abuse) × Loss if abusive

Expected Intervention Cost = Verification Cost + Friction Cost + Goodwill Loss

Rule: If Expected Fraud Loss > Expected Intervention Cost → INTERVENE. Otherwise, allow or use softer friction.

Expected Total Loss(t) = C_FP × FP(t) + C_FN × FN(t)

  • C_FP (£50): £15 support escalation + £35 churn/goodwill penalty.
  • C_FN (£25): £7 reverse courier + £6 warehouse QC + £12 restocking.
  • Optimal Cutoff (t*): 0.65 | Min Expected Loss: £13,950 (model misclassification loss baseline).
FraudGuard does not optimize for maximum fraud detection. It optimizes for maximum expected business value with minimum unnecessary customer friction.
