Dynamic Customer × Product Policy Matrix
Tiered Operational Interventions
Green Tier Zero-Friction Fast-Track (Low Risk Customers / Standard Goods)

Instant refund upon first carrier scan. Retains top 80% customer goodwill without burdening reverse logistics.

Amber Tier Soft Friction & Micro-Fees (Moderate Risk / High Window)

Enforce return window reduction from 30 to 14 days; assess a £3.99 restocking fee on returned orders exceeding 4 items.

Red Tier Hard Verification & Gatekeeping (Chronic Abusers / Wardrobing)

Revoke free home pickup; mandate physical drop-off with 360-degree security tag verification before funds release.

1. Customer-Centric Policy Differentiation
  • VIP Protection: Never apply friction to high-LTV customers on their first rare return; preserve brand affinity.
  • Bracketing Dissuasion: When multiple sizes of the exact same SKU are ordered, introduce virtual sizing assistance prompts before checkout.
  • Identity Linking: Cross-reference device fingerprinting and payment details to avoid repeat abusers registering new accounts.
2. Product & Category-Specific Rules
  • Occasionwear Wardrobing: Attach prominent, non-removable external security ribbons that prevent wearing the garment without removal.
  • High-Value Electronics: Serial number validation matched at warehouse intake before automated refund execution.
  • Low-Ticket Items (<£10): Trigger "Returnless Refunds" for trusted profiles to eliminate reverse shipping waste.
