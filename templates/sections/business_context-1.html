1. Business Context & Industry Background Executive Overview
The E-Commerce Return & Fraud Epidemic

E-commerce return rates have escalated to 24.5% (nearly 3× higher than physical in-store return rates of 8.7%). Customer-friendly, "no-questions-asked" return policies—originally designed to drive digital sales conversion—have become heavily exploited by organized refund syndicates and opportunistic shoppers engaging in wardrobing, synthetic item-not-received claims, and counterfeit tag swaps.

Blanket aggressive crackdowns (such as universally eliminating free returns) risk alienating high-lifetime-value (LTV) loyalists. This platform delivers a dual-tier machine learning architecture: automating instant, zero-friction refunds for verified legitimate customers while precisely intercepting high-risk returns before unrecoverable reverse logistics costs are incurred.
