TF-IDF Mathematical Logic & Strategy

Raw word counts overestimate common conversational words like "item" or "ordered" that contain zero fraud signal.

TF-IDF (TF × IDF) downweights terms appearing across all records while amplifying specific, high-intent keywords (e.g., "wrong", "damaged") that separate genuine fit issues from systematic abuse.
