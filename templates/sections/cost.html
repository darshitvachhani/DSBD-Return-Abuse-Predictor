Cost Optimization Formula & Core Assumptions

1. Calculation Methodology: Standard classification defaults to a 0.50 probability cutoff, which implicitly treats False Positives and False Negatives as equally costly. In commercial operations, misclassifications carry asymmetric penalties. The optimal cutoff t* is computed by iterating threshold t ∈ [0.05, 0.95] to minimize total loss:

Expected Total Loss(t) = (C_FP × FP(t)) + (C_FN × FN(t))

2. Operational Cost Assumptions:

  • False Positive (£50.00 Penalty): Wrongfully inspecting or rejecting a legitimate customer creates support tickets (£15/ticket) and long-term lifetime customer churn risk (£35 amortized).
  • False Negative (£25.00 Penalty): Failing to flag an abusive return results in unrecoverable two-way shipping (£7.00), warehouse item inspection (£6.00), and retail depreciation (£12.00).
