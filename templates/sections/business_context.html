1. Business Problem & Strategic Framework
The Challenge: Abuse vs. Churn Risk

Returns generate massive operational friction. Blanket return friction alienates high-value legitimate customers. Machine learning creates dynamic, risk-tiered policy interventions.

Core Analytical Objectives
  • Supervised Classification (Logistic, CART, Random Forest, Bagging).
  • Unstructured Text Feature Extraction via TF-IDF Vectorization.
  • Customer Segmentation via K-Means Clustering and 2D PCA.
  • Threshold Calibration under Asymmetric Business Misclassification Costs.
