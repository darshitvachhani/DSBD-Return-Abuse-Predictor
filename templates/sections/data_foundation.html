Benchmark Source: UCI Online Retail II

Derived from the standard UCI Online Retail II Data Set (donated by Dr. Daqing Chen, London South Bank University).

  • Raw Baseline: Transaction logs across UK & international retail cohorts.
  • Data Cleansing: Filtered out wholesale bulk cancellations, negative unit anomalies, and missing IDs.
Shopper Persona Synthesis

Because standard transactional logs omit frontline operational risk signals, auxiliary features were synthesized by mapping behavioral profiles:

  • Bracketing & Wardrobing: Multi-size purchasing, extended delays (>20 days), and high lifetime return rates.
  • Device & Metadata: Device fingerprinting and address matches tied to historical velocity.
Text Transformation (TF-IDF)

Free-text return comments (e.g., "arrived damaged", "wrong color") were vectorized using TF-IDF (fit strictly on the training partition).

What is TF-IDF in simple terms? Think of it as a smart filter: it ignores repetitive filler words that everyone uses (like "item" or "ordered") and gives higher importance to rare, informative words (like "damaged" or "wrong") that actually help the model spot fraud.
